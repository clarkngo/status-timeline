3 Principles to Anchor the Strategy
01 / Core Engine
The I-130 is filed immediately after the wedding
Filing the I-130 the week of July 19 is what makes all four scenarios possible. It acts as a permanent anchor in the immigration system — locking in the July 2026 Priority Date. Without it, no path exists.
02 / The Shield
F-1 status is the active legal shield
In Scenarios 1, 2, and 4, keeping student status active — and keeping the green card EAD in a drawer, unused — is the physical shield. Even if stateside I-485 fails, Clark remains 100% legal and can pivot cleanly.
03 / The Reset
Consular processing is the clean path forward
Scenario 1 is the fastest route (~2.5 years) with absolute legal protection from the H-1B gap history. A 2-week Manila trip is the only cost. It is also the recovery path if Scenario 3 or 4 play out.
